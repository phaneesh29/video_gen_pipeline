How ChatGPT Works: Predicting the Next Word (Beginner Layman Guide)
Format: 9:16 Vertical Shorts (Full-screen visual architecture whiteboard).
Visual Architecture: Clean, intuitive 3 to 4 component flow:
- User Prompt (Incoming Tokens)
- LLM Neural Core (Context & Probability Scorer)
- Word Sampler (Picking the next best word)
- Generated Output (The Autocomplete Loop)

Concept & Storyboard Guide for Laymen:
1. The Setup: ChatGPT seems like magic, but at its heart, it is the world's smartest autocomplete engine.
2. Tokenization: When you type a prompt, it breaks your sentence into small bite-sized pieces called tokens, like Lego blocks.
3. Reading Context: The model scans all the previous words together to understand the topic and meaning.
4. Predicting Odds: Instead of thinking like a human, it calculates probability percentages for every possible next word in the dictionary.
5. Example: If the prompt is "The sky is...", it scores "blue" at 85 percent, "dark" at 10 percent, and "pizza" at zero percent.
6. The Feedback Loop: It picks the winning word, appends it to the sentence, and repeats the exact same calculation for the next word until the response is done!
