WebRTC Selective Forwarding Unit (SFU) Star Architecture.
Explain how SFU scales multi-party video conferencing (like Zoom, Google Meet, Discord):
1. Mesh vs SFU: Why P2P mesh collapses due to N*(N-1) uplink saturation, and how SFU creates a scalable Star Topology.
2. Central SFU Media Hub: Publisher client sends a single 1080p uplink stream directly to the central SFU server.
3. Selective RTP Forwarding: Central SFU inspects packet headers and selectively forwards streams to downstream subscribers without expensive decoding or re-encoding.
4. Simulcast Layer Switching: Broadcaster uploads spatial layers (1080p, 720p, 360p); SFU dynamically routes the best layer to each subscriber based on their network speed.
