Title: Hosting AI at Home: Student Expectation vs Reality
Format: 9:16 Vertical Shorts.

Topic & Vibe:
A relatable, humorous take on every student's dream of hosting AI locally at home. Students think: just buy a gaming GPU, host your own LLM, no more subscriptions, and life is pure zingalala! But then the brutal reality of VRAM limits, jet-engine fan noise, and electric bills hits. Keep it funny, fast-paced, and punchy.

Core Story Progression:
1. The Zingalala Dream: Every student thinks: just buy a graphics card, host your own AI, and life is zingalala.
2. The RTX Unboxing: You unbox a shiny RTX graphics card, feeling like Tony Stark setting up your personal Jarvis.
3. The CUDA Crash: You download a massive open source model, hit run, and instantly get slammed with CUDA Out of Memory.
4. The Brutal VRAM Math: Turns out that model needs forty-eight gigabytes of memory, but your student budget only afforded eight.
5. The Bedroom Space Heater: When it finally runs, the cooling fans scream like a jet engine, turning your room into a sauna.
6. The Ollama Rescue: You discover Ollama and four-bit quantization, finally getting fast, intelligent replies completely offline.
7. The Electric Bill Finale: Your local AI dream is finally alive, right until your parents see the massive electricity bill.
