Live Video Streaming Architecture: Ingestion, Multi-CDN Distribution, and Latency Bottlenecks.
Format: 9:16 Vertical Shorts (Full-screen visual architecture diagram).
Architecture Style: Clean, minimal diagram with 3 to 4 distinct key components (Broadcaster, Media Server, CDN Edge, and 1 or 2 Viewers). Never generate duplicate clone boxes!

1. Ingestion: Broadcaster pushes raw video feed to the Origin Media Server using RTMP, SRT, or WebRTC.
2. Transcoding: Media Server transcodes raw streams into Multi-Bitrate ABR ladders (1080p, 720p, 360p) and packages chunks into HLS or DASH.
3. Edge Distribution: CDN Edge Servers cache video segments at edge points of presence, serving downstream viewers worldwide without overloading origin.
4. Scale vs Mesh: Why HTTP chunk caching allows broadcast scale to millions of viewers, unlike peer-to-peer WebRTC meshes.
5. Latency Issues: Chunk duration buffers cause 15 to 30 second latency in standard HLS; Low-Latency HLS drops it to 2 to 4 seconds, trading off caching complexity.
