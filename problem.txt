How YouTube Handles Millions of Video Uploads (System Design Layman Guide)
Format: 9:16 Vertical Shorts (Full-screen visual architecture whiteboard).
No code lines needed, pure system architecture and visual data structures.

Core Architecture Components:
- Client Ingest: Chunked resumable upload over H T T P
- Blob Storage & Message Queue: Google Colossus landing storage and distributed event queue
- Transcoding Fleet: Parallel V C U encoding into 5-second segments, multiple resolutions, and codecs
- Content ID & AI: Audio fingerprinting, copyright scanning, and automated subtitle generation
- Adaptive Bitrate Packaging: H L S and D A S H manifest generation
- Global Edge C D N: Google Global Cache servers inside local internet providers

Concept & Scene Flow:
1. The Scale: Over 500 hours of video are uploaded every single minute. A single machine would melt instantly.
2. Chunked Upload: Your device splits the video into small chunks. If your Wi-Fi drops, it resumes right where it stopped.
3. Queue & Orchestration: Chunks land in raw storage while a message queue splits the job into parallel micro tasks.
4. Parallel Transcoding: The video is cut into 5-second pieces. Hundreds of servers encode them simultaneously into 1080p and 4K.
5. Multi-Codec Packaging: Custom Google chips compress footage into A V 1, V P 9, and H 264 for every device.
6. Content ID & AI Checks: Automated fingerprinting checks for copyright violations while AI transcribes speech into captions.
7. Adaptive Bitrate Streaming: YouTube builds H L S and D A S H playlists so players switch quality seamlessly without buffering.
8. Global Edge Delivery: Videos are cached on Google Global Cache edge servers close to your house for instant streaming.
