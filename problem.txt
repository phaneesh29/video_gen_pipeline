How Netflix Streams 4K Video Without Buffering (System Design Explained)
Format: 9:16 Vertical Shorts (Full-screen visual architecture whiteboard).
No code lines needed, pure system architecture, distributed systems, and visual data structures.

Core Architecture Components:
- Client App: 4K Smart TVs and mobile clients negotiating adaptive bitrate streams
- Control Plane & DRM Gate: Diamond decision checkpoint verifying licenses and 4K capability
- Parallel Transcoding Fleet: Hexagon worker microservices encoding raw footage into hundreds of chunk profiles
- Adaptive Bitrate Engine: Funnel selecting the optimal video quality based on real-time network throughput
- State & Catalog Store: Cylinder databases holding user viewing bookmarks and metadata
- Open Connect C D N: Custom caching servers deployed directly inside local internet providers

Concept & Scene Flow:
1. The 4K Streaming Challenge: Streaming uncompressed 4K video requires massive bandwidth. Netflix must deliver pristine ultra high definition instantly without a single second of buffering.
2. The License Check: Before a single frame plays, a diamond decision gate verifies your device D R M and 4K subscription authorization.
3. Massive Encoding Fleet: Raw film footage is sliced into tiny segments. A cluster of hexagon worker nodes compresses each scene into dozens of resolutions using A V 1 and H E V C codecs.
4. Adaptive Bitrate Selection: Your device constantly measures connection speed, dynamically choosing whether to pull 4K, 1080p, or 720p chunks as your Wi-Fi fluctuates.
5. In-Memory State Sync: A distributed cylinder database updates your watch history and timestamp in milliseconds so you can resume on another screen.
6. Open Connect Edge Delivery: Instead of pulling video across the globe, Netflix stores popular movies on custom caching servers placed directly inside your local internet service provider.
7. Seamless 4K Playback: The combination of local edge caching, parallel chunk encoding, and intelligent bitrate switching delivers crystal-clear 4K video instantly.
